USYS ID Camp · 2026

A forward who presses, creates, and puts it away.

Amaya is a 2012-born forward for Chicago Inter, playing the game off her left foot with the kind of motor that changes a match before the scoreboard does. She hunts the first defender, makes the runs in behind, and arrives in the box on time.

Her game is built on relentless work-rate and final-third decision-making — equally comfortable peeling off the shoulder to finish or dropping in to combine and create for a teammate. In 2026 she was invited to the USYS National ID Camp in Orlando, and she has been selected to the ODP Illinois State Team two years running.
